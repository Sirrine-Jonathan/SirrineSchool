Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

Sirrine School is a learning application designed for children. We take your privacy and your child's safety very seriously.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal information from our users. We do not require account creation, and we do not store any identifying information on our servers.

2. Local Storage

The application uses local browser storage (localStorage) on your device to keep track of game progress, XP, and mastered games. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

3. Advertisements and Analytics

Sirrine School is 100% ad-free. We do not use any third-party tracking or analytics services.

4. COPPA Compliance

Our application is designed to be fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect, use, or disclose personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
